Three rules under all of it.

Whatever the tool, whatever the team, these don't change. They're the difference between AI that helps and AI that gets us in trouble.

Enterprise, not personal

Client data only goes into vetted, enterprise accounts. Never a free personal login, that's where the leaks happen.

Verify everything

Facts, figures, quotes, sources. AI is confident even when it's wrong. If you'd check it from a junior, check it from AI.

Human signs off

Nothing client-facing ships without a person approving it. AI accelerates the work, it doesn't take responsibility for it.
